BACK BUTTON: LEVEL SELECT, THEN CONFIGURATION
===========================================================================

You: "change the game so that during the level if you press back on the
firestick remote it goes back to level select and if you press back on the
level select it goes back to configuration."

DURING A LEVEL
   Back now goes straight to the era picker — no pause stop-off first. You
   can still pause: the phone's own pause button (and solo mode's on-screen
   one) still work exactly as before. It's just that Back on the remote is
   now a one-press "get me out of here", not "pause, then a second press to
   quit".

ON THE ERA PICKER
   One more Back press sends every connected phone back to choosing motion
   camera or pad controls, then recalibrating from scratch — the same
   placement, framing, per-move walkthrough and review as the very first
   time. The TV drops back to the "waiting for setup" Ready screen while
   that happens. This is a genuine do-over: if you'd already finished
   setting up, that's forgotten, and you'll need to finish it again before
   you can start a run.

   Everyone in the room gets sent back together — the TV has no way to know
   it was only one of several players who wanted to reconfigure, so a Back
   press here starts the whole room over, not just one player.

Testing: new suite (12/12) covering Back mid-run landing on the era picker
without a pause screen in between, Back while already paused still reaching
it too (unchanged), and Back on the era picker resetting who's finished
setup and telling the phone to redo it. Full regression re-run against the
existing setup/calibration/medieval-era suites — no new failures.
