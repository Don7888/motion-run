This round — four fixes from Don's report:

1. EASY / MEDIUM / HARD DIFFICULTY
   New setting, top-right corner badge on every screen (click to cycle),
   or the remote's Up/Down while on the very first "Ready?" screen (before
   setup starts). Medium is exactly the game as it always played — nothing
   changes if you never touch this. Easy is noticeably slower, spaces
   obstacles out more, barely speeds up over a long run, and turns the
   Dino era's rolling lava boulder off entirely (the example Don gave for
   "too much for a 4 year old"). Hard is a moderate step up from Medium.
   Your choice is remembered (like the mute setting).

2. CAMERA TRACKING — stepping toward/away from the phone mid-run
   Previously, moving noticeably closer to or further from the phone
   during a run could throw off the character's left/right and jump/duck
   tracking, because the tracker's reference points were only ever set
   once, at the start. Now this is checked continuously: if you drift too
   close or too far, the game notices immediately, re-anchors itself to
   your new position, and puts a red "Take a step back" / "Come a bit
   closer" banner on the TV so you know to reposition.

3. MUSIC NOT PLAYING WHEN A LEVEL IS PICKED
   Confirmed as a real browser restriction: if a whole session is played
   entirely from the phone (the physical Fire TV remote is never pressed
   even once), the browser refuses to start any audio at all — this is a
   deliberate browser rule, not a bug in the usual sense, and there's no
   way to force it. What WAS a bug is that the only on-screen sign this had
   happened was a small label tucked in the bottom-left corner, easy to
   miss from across a room. It's now a large banner across the
   bottom-centre of the screen: "🔊 Press OK on your remote for sound" —
   one press of the actual remote (any button) is all it takes to unlock
   sound for the rest of the session.

4. QUITTING A LEVEL TO PICK ANOTHER
   Pressing Back on the remote (or Exit on the phone) during a run now
   goes straight to the era picker. It used to take three Back presses and
   land you back on a screen that only let you replay the SAME level —
   now it's two presses (pause, then quit) and you're on the picker.

Files changed this round: public/tv/game.js, public/tv/index.html,
public/play/controller.js. (public/play/index.html unchanged.)

Testing: full existing regression suite re-run clean, plus four new test
files covering all four fixes above. The only failures are two known,
pre-existing, unrelated ones in the QR-code test (a test-detection quirk,
not a real bug) that predate this round.
