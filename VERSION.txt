What's in this round: menu clarity fixes, from Don's screenshots
("Mobile: numbers are on top of the 0s" / "Tv: the menu is so unclear")

  1. Phone join screen — the 6-digit code box was see-through art with
     "000000" painted into it underneath, and a typed digit didn't
     cleanly replace the baked zero, so you saw both at once. Fixed:
     the code box now paints its own solid white background over that
     spot, so only what you actually type is ever shown.
  2. TV Ready screen — the "Setup 1/4 ..." banner at the top was long
     enough to wrap onto two lines and sit on top of the artwork's own
     header. Shortened it and gave it a bit more room. It also used to
     repeat "press OK to start" a second time lower on the screen even
     when there was nothing more to say — that line now stays quiet
     unless it actually has something new to add (like "more than one
     player" controls, or who's still finishing setup).

public/tv/index.html
