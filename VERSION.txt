WHAT THE THREE ROUNDS CONTAIN
===========================================================================

ROUND 3 — 15 Sep (commit 82af736)

1. MOTION TRACKING NOW WORKS WITHOUT SEEING YOUR WHOLE BODY
   You: "In landscape it's rare that the players full body will be
   visible."

   It was worse than just less accurate: the tracking needed to see your
   shoulders AND your hips before it would register anything at all.
   Propped up in landscape it often sees you from the waist up, and in
   that case nothing was ever detected. The setup screen also told you to
   move further back whenever you filled more of the picture than a
   full-body shot — which is exactly the position that tracks best, so
   you could get stuck being told to back away from a spot that would
   have worked.

   It now works from your head and shoulders alone. If it can see your
   hips it still uses them; if it can't, it measures from your shoulders
   instead. Losing sight of your hips mid-game no longer causes a phantom
   jump, and hips flickering in and out at the bottom of the picture no
   longer make it keep re-adjusting. The "are you in shot?" check now
   asks whether you're big enough to read and whether you're being cut
   off — including whether there's room above your head to jump into.

2. EVERY SETUP MOVE IS COUNTED IN
   "Get ready… 3, 2, 1, GO" before each move. The phone genuinely isn't
   listening during the count, so it can't tick a move off while you're
   still walking back into position.

3. YOUR CHARACTER COPIES YOU THROUGHOUT SETUP
   Your character is on screen for the whole of setup doing whatever you
   do — including moves the walkthrough hasn't asked for yet. Shown from
   behind, the same view as the game, so left and right mean the same
   thing in setup as in play.

4. A REVIEW AT THE END
   Setup finishes on a list of all five moves showing which registered.
   ▲▼ to pick one and OK to do it again, or pick "Start playing". If
   something didn't work it's already selected — one press of OK redoes
   it.

5. A MOVE THAT WON'T REGISTER NO LONGER TRAPS SETUP
   Press ▶ (right) on the remote to skip it; it gets flagged on the
   review list. OK still means "finish setup and start".

ROUND 2 — 14 Sep (commit 387b8db)

6. iPHONE CAMERA MODE — THE PHONE WAS FALLING ASLEEP
   You: "iPhone 13 didn't work when doing the motion capture."

   Camera mode asks you to prop the phone up and never touch it again,
   so it locks its own screen — and iOS shuts the camera off when it
   does. Everything silently stopped from that moment; pick the phone up
   and the camera looks perfectly healthy again, which is why it was hard
   to spot. An iPhone's Auto-Lock can be as short as 30 seconds. Android
   was getting away with it by accident.

   The game now holds the screen awake while the camera is running. On
   iPhones too old to allow that (before iOS 16.4) it tells you to set
   Auto-Lock to Never first.

7. WHEN TRACKING STOPS, YOU GET TOLD
   Previously a dead tracker looked identical to one that just couldn't
   see you. Now the phone retries the camera by itself, and if that
   fails the TV says "Phone stopped tracking — wake it up".

8. A "TRACKING DETAILS" READOUT ON THE PHONE
   Tap it to see what tracking is doing — camera resolution, speed,
   whether it can see your body, whether the screen is being held awake,
   last error. It opens itself if tracking fails. If a phone misbehaves
   again, one photo of that panel should explain why.

9. Fixed: on iPhone, a camera problem used to silently kill hold-phone
   mode too, leaving nothing working. There's now a "Turn on motion
   sensors" button when that happens.
