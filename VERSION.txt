THE THREE THINGS YOU REPORTED
===========================================================================

1. BACK NO LONGER LEAVES THE APP
   Two separate faults were behind this, and both are fixed.

   The game was reacting to Back correctly but never telling the browser it
   had dealt with it — so the app went to the era picker AND closed itself
   at the same time. On top of that, the Fire TV app is a web-app shell,
   and its Back arrives as a page navigation rather than a button press, so
   on some builds the game never saw it at all. It now catches both, and
   holds a spare history entry so a Back press has something to consume
   other than the app itself.

   Back now walks back one screen at a time:
     during a run            -> era picker
     results / leaderboard   -> era picker
     calibration review      -> back into the last move
     a move                  -> the previous move
     first move              -> the framing screen
     framing                 -> the "place your phone" screen
     placement               -> the pre-setup Ready screen
     era picker (home)       -> a menu: Keep playing / Change controls and
                                redo setup / Quit MotionQuest

   The menu always opens on "Keep playing", so a stray OK press can never
   quit, and Back while it is open just closes it. Quitting the app now
   takes a deliberate arrow-down-twice-then-OK. "Change controls / redo
   setup" is where the behaviour you asked for last night now lives — it
   is still there, it just isn't what a bare Back press does any more.

2. PUNCH FIRING WHEN YOU HAVEN'T PUNCHED
   The bar was raised (a punch now has to reach further and move faster,
   and can't re-fire for 900ms), but the real fix is two new requirements
   that aren't about sensitivity at all:

   - A punch has to be EXTENDING. Your fist has to be getting further from
     your shoulder. An arm swinging while you step sideways moves every bit
     as fast as a punch, which is why speed alone kept firing — but it
     doesn't extend, so it's now rejected outright.
   - A punch has to be roughly FORWARD, not vertical. Arms flying up as you
     land a jump, or reaching up, were the other big source.

   In hold-the-phone mode there was a genuine logic bug: on any phone that
   doesn't report rotation data (most Androids), practically any sharp
   movement that wasn't big enough to count as a jump fell through and
   fired a punch instead. That's fixed.

   And the loosened "make punch easy to land" thresholds that setup uses
   now apply ONLY while setup is actually asking for a punch. They were
   live through the whole of setup before, which is why stepping sideways
   in front of the camera registered as punches.

3. THE CHARACTER NOW DEMONSTRATES, THEN COPIES
   During each move's count-in the character performs the move itself —
   twice — then returns to standing still with about a second to spare, so
   you're not copying a moving target. Only after GO does it start copying
   you, and only for the move being asked for. Nothing you do during the
   count-in moves it at all. That's what stops the character punching and
   stepping about before those moves have been configured.

   NEW: a live figure of you in the bottom-right corner during setup.
   Three columns show the LEFT / MIDDLE / RIGHT zones with the one you're
   standing in lit up, and the two dividing lines show exactly how far you
   have to move to change lane — which was invisible before. If you drift
   out of shot it says so ("Come a bit closer" / "Take a step back" /
   "Step back into view"), and any limb outside the picture is marked.

   It's drawn from the body points your phone already tracks, not camera
   video — same information, a fraction of the network traffic, and no
   picture of your living room ever leaves the phone.
