overwritten with the complete, tested "public" tree — so you never have to
guess which of your Desktop folders is current.

How to use this folder
-----------------------
1. Open this folder's "public" subfolder.
2. Drag the whole "public" folder onto:
   https://github.com/Don7888/motion-run/upload/main
3. Click "Commit changes".
4. Tell Claude it's uploaded — it will verify the upload matches this
   build and trigger the live deploy.

Your other Desktop folders (motionquest-round2, motionquest-update,
motionquest-upload, motionquest-join-screen-update, motionquest,
motion-run, motion-run-icons, motion-run-lan) are all leftovers from
earlier rounds. They are now obsolete — everything in them is already
included here, up to date. Safe to delete once this one is confirmed
uploaded, to stop the desktop clutter that caused a bad upload before.
